📖 Algorithm Descriptions

🎲 Pure Random

Uniform random selection from all Beys. No constraints. Ideal for chaos tournaments or casual play.

📊 Ranking Bucket Balanced

Splits leaderboard into ranking ranges (Top, Mid, Bottom) and draws roughly equal numbers from each. Creates fairer brackets.

⚖️ Weighted by Elo

Higher Elo = higher selection probability. Strong Beys appear more often, but surprises still possible.

🎯 Type-Based Distribution

Ensures balanced mix of Attack, Defense, Stamina, and Balance types. Prevents type-heavy tournaments.

🎭 Archetype-Based Distribution

Ensures diversity across playstyles and archetypes. Uses RPG stats for variety.

⚙️ Custom Constraints

Advanced filtering with min/max Elo ranges, include/exclude lists, and custom rules.
