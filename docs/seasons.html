About the Seasonal League System

The Seasonal League is a tiered competition system where 40 Beyblades compete across 4 tiers. Each season features round-robin matches within tiers, followed by promotion/relegation and a post-season cup tournament.

Competition Format

🎯 League Matches

  • Single round-robin within each tier
  • 9 matches per Bey
  • Win: 3 points
  • Dominant Win (4-0+): 4 points

↕️ Promotion & Relegation

  • Top 2: Automatic promotion
  • Bottom 2: Automatic relegation
  • 8th vs 3rd: Relegation match

🏆 Season Cup

  • Top 4 from Tier I
  • Top 2 from Tier II
  • Top 1 from Tier III & IV
  • Double-elimination format

📊 League Table Ranking

  1. Season Points
  2. Point Difference
  3. Total Points Scored
  4. Head-to-Head
  5. ELO Rating
