About the Seasonal League System

The Seasonal League is a tiered competition system where 32 Beyblades compete across 4 tiers. Each season features round-robin matches within tiers, followed by promotion/relegation matches. Tier IV acts as the Challengers Tier: its bottom 4 Beys drop into the Qualification Pool, while unranked Beys compete in a Qualification Tournament for Tier IV slots.

Competition Format

🎯 League Matches

  • Single round-robin within each tier
  • 7 matches per Bey
  • Win: 3 points
  • Dominant Win (4-0+): 4 points

↕️ Promotion & Relegation

  • T1: Rank 8 relegated; Rank 7 playoff
  • T2: Rank 1 promoted; Ranks 7–8 relegated; Rank 2 & 6 playoff
  • T3: Ranks 1–2 promoted; Ranks 7–8 relegated; Rank 3 & 6 playoff
  • T4: Ranks 1–2 promoted; Rank 3 playoff; Ranks 5–8 → Qual. Pool

🏆 Season Cup

  • Top 4 from Tier I
  • Top 2 from Tier II
  • Top 1 from Tier III
  • Top 1 from Tier IV
  • Double-elimination format

📊 League Table Ranking

  1. Season Points
  2. Point Difference
  3. Total Points Scored
  4. Head-to-Head
  5. ELO Rating
