📜 Ruleset Archive & History

A complete, season-by-season record of the Seasonal League format: league structure, scoring, promotion & relegation rules, the Season Cup, and every change between seasons.

📖 About this archive

The Seasonal League has evolved since its launch. Season 1 used a smaller 3-tier format that was deemed too "top-heavy" (one dominant tier absorbed the best beys with little churn). From Season 2 onward the league expanded to a 4-tier pyramid with a dedicated Qualification Pool feeding the bottom tier — a structure that has remained in place through Season 3.

Use the timeline below to jump to any season, or scroll through for a full side-by-side comparison. The Current badge marks the ruleset that is in effect for the active (or upcoming) season.

🧠 Change highlights

🆚 S1 → S2 (the big reformat)

The single most impactful change in league history. Going from a 3-tier flat league to a 4-tier pyramid introduced a true promotion pathway from the bottom up, gave weaker beys more regular matches, and added relegation playoffs at every boundary to keep tier movement meaningful.

Added Tier IV as a dedicated "Challengers Tier" with 8 slots fed by a Qualification Pool.
Changed Tier size reduced from 10 → 8 beys, dropping the season's match count from 135 to 112 while still covering every pairing (round-robin).
Changed Promotion / relegation is now tier-scaled (1/2/2 for promotions; 1/2/2 for relegations) rather than a flat "top 2 / bottom 2".
Added Relegation playoffs at the T3/T4 boundary in addition to T1/T2 and T2/T3 (three pairs total).
Added Season Cup — an 8-bey double-elimination tournament with qualification slots for every tier (4-2-1-1).
Changed Qualification pool now feeds only the bottom tier (T4), with pool slots filled by ELO order from the previous pool + new qualification tournament winners.

🔁 S2 → S3 (year-2 continuation)

No structural changes — Season 3 is a continuation of the v2 ruleset introduced in S2. Adjustments were operational only.

Note Same 4×8 format, same promotion/relegation counts, same Season Cup structure (4-2-1-1, double-elimination).
Note Tier composition was rebuilt from S2's promotion/relegation results via initialize_season_from_results() rather than the original ELO-snapshot initialisation.
Note The qualification pool retained its role as the gap-filler for T4 vacancies, with qualification-candidates competing for re-entry.
